## 🎯 What is BMAD-METHOD™?

BMAD (Breakthrough Method of Agile AI-Driven Development) is a **Universal AI Agent Framework** that transforms development with specialized AI expertise through:

1. **Agentic Planning** - Analyst, PM, and Architect agents create detailed PRDs and Architecture
2. **Context-Engineered Development** - Scrum Master generates hyper-detailed stories for Dev agents
3. **Specialized Agents** - QA, DevOps, Security, and more for comprehensive coverage
