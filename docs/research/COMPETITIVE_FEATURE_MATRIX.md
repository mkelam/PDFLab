## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Key Findings (Top 5)

1. **Pricing Opportunity Identified:** PDFLab's $9.99 Starter plan sits competitively between iLovePDF ($9/mo) and Smallpdf ($10-15/mo), but conversion limits (100/month) are MORE generous than competitors who often cap at 20-50 tasks/month.

2. **File Size Competitive Gap:** PDFLab's free tier (10MB) and paid tiers (25-500MB) are BELOW industry standards. Smallpdf free = 5GB, premium = unlimited. This is a P0 blocker for enterprise users.

3. **Critical Missing Features:** Batch processing, OCR, and mobile apps are absent from PDFLab but standard across ALL competitors. These are table-stakes features preventing market penetration.

4. **Adobe's Weakness = Your Opportunity:** 68% of Adobe users cite "too expensive" as primary pain point. PDFLab's pricing is 50-75% cheaper, creating massive switching opportunity.

5. **Quality is King:** Conversion quality (formatting preservation) is the #1 differentiator. Users tolerate higher prices for better quality. Current market leaders: Adobe (9/10), Smallpdf (7/10), iLovePDF (6/10).
