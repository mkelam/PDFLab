## Red Flags to Avoid

**Don't Partner With:**
1. **Fake Engagement:** Comments like "Nice post!" with no context
2. **Follower Ratio Issues:** 10k followers, 50 likes per post = fake followers
3. **Off-Brand Content:** Influencer promotes scammy products or unrelated niches
4. **Poor Content Quality:** Blurry photos, typos, unprofessional presentation
5. **No Engagement:** High follower count, zero comments or discussion

**Green Flags (Good Signs):**
1. **Authentic Comments:** "Thanks for sharing! I use [similar tool]"
2. **High Saves/Shares:** Indicates valuable content people want to reference later
3. **Consistent Posting:** Active account, regular content schedule
4. **Niche Expertise:** Deep knowledge in productivity, not generic lifestyle content
5. **Audience Questions:** Followers actively asking for recommendations
