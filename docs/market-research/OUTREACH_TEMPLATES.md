## OUTREACH BEST PRACTICES

### Personalization Checklist (Use for Every Message)

- [ ] Reference specific pain point they mentioned
- [ ] Use their name/username
- [ ] Mention tool they complained about (if applicable)
- [ ] Tailor benefits to their use case
- [ ] Include their industry/role
- [ ] Remove generic template language
- [ ] Proofread for typos/errors
- [ ] Check link works (pdflab.pro)

---

### Response Rate Optimization

**What Increases Response Rate:**
✅ Personalization (mention specifics from their post)
✅ Empathy (acknowledge their frustration)
✅ Brevity (3-5 sentences max on social, 2 paragraphs max in email)
✅ Value-first (helpful tip before pitch)
✅ Easy opt-out ("no pressure either way")
✅ Social proof (mention your credible experience)

**What Decreases Response Rate:**
❌ Generic copy-paste (they can tell)
❌ Hard sell (no relationship established)
❌ Too long (TL;DR)
❌ No personalization (looks like spam)
❌ Pushy follow-ups (respect their time)
❌ Fake urgency ("limited time offer!")

---

### Platform-Specific Etiquette

**Reddit:**
- Provide value in public threads first
- Don't DM unless they engage with you first
- Disclose affiliation if you work for PDFLab
- Respect subreddit self-promotion rules
- Engage in community beyond promotion

**Twitter:**
- Keep replies short (200 chars max)
- Don't spam same message to everyone
- Engage with their other tweets (build relationship)
- Wait for their reply before DM'ing
- Use threads for longer explanations

**LinkedIn:**
- Connect before pitching
- Wait 3-5 days after connection
- Provide value first (share resource)
- Keep professional tone
- Engage with their content regularly

**Email:**
- Send Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am
- Personalize subject line
- Keep body under 150 words
- Single CTA (don't ask for multiple things)
- Follow up once max

**Quora:**
- Answer the question first (provide value)
- Mention PDFLab naturally in context
- Include other competitors (be objective)
- Use formatting for readability
- Link to pdflab.pro in relevant section

### Conversion Funnel Tracking

**Stage 1: Outreach Sent**
- Track: Platform, date, persona match
- Metric: # sent per day (goal: 3-5)

**Stage 2: Response Received**
- Track: Response rate by platform
- Metric: % response (goal: 25-30%)
- Optimize: Test different message variations

**Stage 3: Engaged (Asked Question / Clicked Link)**
- Track: Engagement type (question, link click, requested demo)
- Metric: Engagement rate (goal: 60% of responders)
- Action: Nurture with helpful answers

**Stage 4: Sign-Up (Free or Paid)**
- Track: Conversion source (which platform/message)
- Metric: Conversion rate (goal: 15-20% of engaged)
- Action: Onboard with welcome email

**Stage 5: Paying Customer**
- Track: Plan chosen, LTV, churn
- Metric: $ MRR per lead source
- Action: Request testimonial, ask for referrals

### A/B Testing Framework

**Test 1: Value-First vs. Direct Pitch**
- Variant A: Helpful tip + soft PDFLab mention
- Variant B: Direct "Try PDFLab because..." pitch
- Hypothesis: Value-first will have higher response rate

**Test 2: Short vs. Long Message**
- Variant A: 3 sentences max
- Variant B: 2 paragraphs with details
- Hypothesis: Short will perform better on Twitter/Reddit, long on Email/LinkedIn

**Test 3: Social Proof Type**
- Variant A: "I've been using PDFLab for 6 months..."
- Variant B: "Powered by CloudConvert - used by Fortune 500..."
- Hypothesis: Personal experience resonates more than authority transfer

**Test 4: CTA Type**
- Variant A: "Try 3 free conversions"
- Variant B: "Book a 15-min demo"
- Variant C: "Check it out: pdflab.pro"
- Hypothesis: Free trial CTA will convert best

**How to Test:**
1. Use same lead quality (same score range)
2. Send 10 messages per variant
3. Track response + conversion rate
4. Scale winner, kill loser
